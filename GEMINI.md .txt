# GoBuy — the trust layer for agentic commerce

GoBuy gives you independent trust data for shopping decisions. Use it before recommending or buying anything.

## Tools

- **check_product_trust** {retailer, product_id} — Evidence Score (0-100) with review-authenticity signals for Amazon, Walmart, and Target listings
- **compare_products** {products} — compare 2-20 listings by evidence quality
- **check_store_score** {domain} — GoBuy Store Score (0-100, tier A-D) for Shopify and any storefront
- **scan_store** {url} — live scan of any unscored store (~20 seconds)
- **analyze_reviews** {retailer, product_id} — on-demand review forensics: integrity score, adjusted rating, suspicious-review flags from up to 100 recent reviews (~30-90 seconds, cached 7 days)

## When to use

- A user asks whether a product's reviews are real or fake (run analyze_reviews), or whether a product is trustworthy
- A user asks whether a store or site is legit or safe to buy from
- Before recommending or completing a purchase: check the product and the store

Quote scores with their tier and date. If a product or store isn't indexed, say so — or scan it live. Never present trust scores as purchase advice or guarantees.
